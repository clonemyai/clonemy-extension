clonemy.ai

Open one of your AI chats (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Muse, Meta AI) and use the clonemy bar in the corner of the tab. Everything happens in the tab. The one exception is the optional browser sync below, off unless you turn it on.

What this extension knows
A tally of which AI sites you use, counted on this machine only, so it can tell your regular AI from a one-time visit. It never leaves this browser. When you compile a capsule, it also waits here, on this machine only, for up to a day so another AI tab can offer it to you; discard it from the bar at any time. Your latest capsule from each AI is kept in a vault here, versioned, until you forget it from the bar, and the extension notes which version each AI last received so it can offer you updates. If you turn on browser sync, your vault also rides your browser's own sync (Chrome Sync) as encrypted chunks protected by that sync phrase; the carrier sees sizes and timing, never content.

Send to phone
Carry a capsule from your vault to your phone. A tab opens with a QR code; scan it with the phone, and the phone accepts before anything moves.

Browser sync (experimental)
Keep your capsule vault current across your own browsers. Capsules are encrypted with a sync phrase the extension creates for you, and carried by your browser's built-in sync. clonemy runs no server and sees nothing.
